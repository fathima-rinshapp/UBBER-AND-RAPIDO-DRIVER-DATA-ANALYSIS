# UBBER AND RAPIDO DRIVER DATA ANALYSIS & DASHBOARD
 Project Overview
 This is an end-to-end Excel and Power BI project analyzing 46K drivers for Uber & Rapido. The project tracks the complete journey from Interview to Active Driver Performance.
 It answers 3 key business questions:
 Where are our drivers and are they active?
 Who are our best vs worst performers?
 Why are we rejecting 66% of candidates?
 🎯 Problem Statement
 The company had 46K driver records but faced issues:50% drivers inactive
 Average rating only 3.8334% selection rate
 22% document pass rate9K drivers with <50% trip completion
 📊 Dashboards Created (3 + 1 Insights Page)
 Page 1: Executive Overview
 KPIs: Total Drivers 46K, Active 23K, Avg Earning 1.43K, Avg Rating 3.83
 Visuals: Map (Active Driver by City), Platform Split, Rating Distribution, Language Distribution
 Page 2: Driver Performance Analysis
 KPIs: Top Rated 4+ (11K), Low Rated <3 (2K), High Earners >2K (10K), Low Completion <50% (9K)
 Visuals: Trip Completion by Rating, Vehicle Type Donut, Experience vs Platform Treemap, Score vs Age
 Page 3: Interview Results Analysis
 KPIs: Total Interview 46K, Selection Rate 34%, Document Pass 22.79%, Avg Score 6.42
 Visuals: Background Check Donut, Interview Funnel, Rejection Reasons Bar, Platform Comparison
 Page 4: Insights & Recommendations
 Single page consolidated business insights
